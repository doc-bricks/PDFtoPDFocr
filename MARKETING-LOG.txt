2. TARGET PERSONAS & VALUE PROPOSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Persona 1: Legal, Healthcare & Regulatory Compliance Officers
  * Pain: Uploading sensitive patient files, litigation records, or tax receipts to cloud OCR services violates GDPR/HIPAA zero-egress data protection mandates.
  * Solution: 100% local, air-gapped OCR processing where document bytes never leave the local system; non-destructive preserving of original evidence files.
- Persona 2: Archivists, Librarians & Academic Researchers
  * Pain: Large collections of scanned historical manuscripts, multi-frame TIFF images, and multi-lingual books cannot be converted into searchable full-text archives without expensive per-page subscription fees.
  * Solution: High-throughput batch conversion for PDF and image queues (JPG, PNG, multi-page TIFF), selectable language models with auto-download from official Tesseract sources, and automated multi-document PDF merge.
- Persona 3: Privacy-Conscious Knowledge Workers & Solo Business Owners
  * Pain: Desktop tools like Adobe Acrobat or ABBYY FineReader require heavy annual subscriptions, cloud logins, intrusive background update daemons, or administrative elevation.
  * Solution: Free, open-source MIT desktop tool with zero telemetry, unprivileged RunAsInvoker operation, portable single-executable or zero-install folder options, and keyboard-first accessibility.
- Persona 4: Automated Pipeline Integrators & Document Workflow Engineers
  * Pain: GUI applications typically lack structured introspection, making it difficult to verify batch processing outcomes or integrate results with downstream automation.
  * Solution: Structured job manifest export (pdftopdfocr-job-v1.json) capturing processing stats, timestamps, language tags, and page counts; CLI-ready invocation; machine-readable context via llms.txt.
